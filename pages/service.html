Interact Club of Oberoi Garden City

As a founding member of the Interact Club of Oberoi Garden City, I have been part of building a student-led platform for community service and meaningful action over the past four years.

My journey has taken me from Treasurer to Vice President and now President. As Vice President in 2025–26, I helped lead fundraising efforts that raised over ₹45,000 towards club funds and various initiatives. Now, as President for 2026–27, I continue to lead initiatives, bring students together, and create opportunities for impact.
